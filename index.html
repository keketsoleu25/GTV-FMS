🏆 About GTV FMS

Gauteng's Partner for Professional Facilities Management

GTV FMS was established with a single mission: to deliver reliable, high-quality facilities management and cleaning services that help our clients maintain clean, safe, and efficient environments.

We understand that every business is unique. Whether you manage a bustling shopping centre in Johannesburg, a factory in Germiston, or a school in Pretoria, we design bespoke service schedules that keep your space spotless without disrupting your operations.
